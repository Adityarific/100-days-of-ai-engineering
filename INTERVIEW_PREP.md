# Interview Prep

The roadmap ends with [100 interview questions](https://ai-engineer-roadmap-2026.vercel.app/100-days/interview-prep/). They are not copied here. This file tracks which ones I have answered **out loud and in my own words**, with evidence from my own experiments.

## How I use it

1. After finishing a module, answer that module's questions without looking at the outline.
2. Compare with the roadmap's outline of a strong answer and note what I missed.
3. Add one real example from my own experiments (a number, a failure, a fix).
4. Record it: `python scripts/tracker.py answer 1-12` (single numbers and ranges both work).
